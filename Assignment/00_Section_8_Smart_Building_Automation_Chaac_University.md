# Smart Building Automation for Chaac University

## Description

Chaac University, with a 500-acre campus, 35 buildings, and 25,000 students, plans to implement a comprehensive smart building automation system to reduce energy consumption by 30%, improve facility management efficiency, and enhance occupant comfort.

---

## Business Context

Rising energy costs ($2M annually) and sustainability commitments drive the need for smart building technologies. Current building management systems are outdated and fragmented.

---

## In Scope

* Installation of 5,000+ IoT sensors and smart control systems
* Development of centralized management dashboard and mobile app
* Integration with existing building management and HVAC systems
* Predictive maintenance system implementation
* Energy monitoring and optimization algorithms
* User training for 50+ facility management staff
* Student engagement app for sustainability awareness

---

## Out of Scope

* Major building renovations or structural changes
* Non-automation related facility upgrades (furniture, landscaping)
* Dormitory automation (Phase 2 project)

---

## Success Criteria

* 30% reduction in energy consumption within 12 months
* 50% reduction in maintenance response time
* 95% system uptime and reliability
* Carbon footprint reduction of 25%

---

## Final Deliverables

* Fully operational smart building automation system across 35 buildings
* Comprehensive energy efficiency and cost savings report
* System maintenance guide and troubleshooting documentation
* Sustainability impact assessment and future roadmap

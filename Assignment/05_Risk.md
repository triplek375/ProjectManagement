# Assignment 5 – Risk

## Step 1: 
Gather your project data. Compile all the project information your team has developed
so far and provide it to an AI tool of your choice.

## Step 2: 
Generate a risk register with AI. Ask the AI to identify the top 15 risks for your project.
For each risk, the output should include:
1. Priority rating (High / Medium / Low). Your team decides how to define these levels. Briefly explain the criteria you used.
2. Mitigation strategies. Specific actions to reduce the likelihood or impact of the risk.
3. Additional relevant information. Anything else useful, such as risk owner, early warning signs, or contingency plans.

## Step 3: 
Review and improve the output (human in the loop). Paste the AI’s original output into a Word document or Google Doc. Then turn on Track Changes (Word) or Suggesting mode (Google Docs) before making any edits. Revise, correct, and strengthen the AI’s work so your team’s contributions are clearly visible.

## Step 4: 
Submit. Upload the document, or submit a link to it. If you share a link, make sure your instructor has access.

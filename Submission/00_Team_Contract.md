# Team Contract

## Contract

| Section | Agreement / Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Team Name** | Group 8 |
| **Guiding Principles** | We will work collaboratively, communicate respectfully, and contribute fairly toward completing our team's work. We will take responsibility for our contributions and support one another in achieving our shared goals. |
| **Purpose** | We will work together to plan and support the implementation of a smart building automation system for Chaac University. Our goal is to help reduce energy consumption, improve facility management efficiency, and enhance occupant comfort while meeting the project's requirements and success criteria. |
| **Division of Labor** | We will delegate tasks based on the project requirements, individual strengths, and workload to ensure responsibilities are distributed fairly. We will work together to determine appropriate responsibilities and communicate when additional support or adjustments are needed. |
| **Group Roles** | We will divide project roles based on the needs of the project and the strengths of each team member. Roles will include responsibilities related to project planning, research, technical requirements, documentation, and presentation of our work. Team members will work together and provide support when responsibilities overlap or additional help is needed. |
| **Team Plan** | We will establish responsibilities and project deadlines based on the requirements, scope, and deliverables of the project. We will regularly review our progress, communicate about any challenges, and adjust our plan as needed to keep the team on track. |
| **Meetings** | We will meet as needed to discuss project progress, coordinate responsibilities, and address any questions or concerns. Meetings will be scheduled using Microsoft Teams, in the form of virtual meetings or in-person meetings whenever possible. We will make a reasonable effort to attend scheduled meetings and come prepared to contribute. |
| **Communication** | We will use Microsoft Teams as our primary method of group communication, with text messaging used when a more immediate response is needed. We will respond in a timely manner and provide relevant updates regarding our work and overall team progress. |
| **Conduct** | We will treat one another with respect and professionalism. We will listen to different perspectives, remain open to feedback, and contribute positively to our team's working environment. |
| **Conflict** | We will address disagreements openly and respectfully. When conflicts arise, we will discuss the issue as a team, consider different perspectives, and work together toward a fair and practical solution. |
| **Rewards & Punishment** | Team members who consistently contribute, complete assigned work, and communicate effectively will be recognized through strong peer evaluation scores and overall team performance. Team members who do not meet these expectations may receive lower peer evaluation scores based on their contributions and participation. |
| **Provisions for Changing the Contract** | Changes to this contract can be proposed by any team member and discussed openly during a scheduled team meeting. Amendments to the contract require the unanimous consent of all team members before taking effect. |

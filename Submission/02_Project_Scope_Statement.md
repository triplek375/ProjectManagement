# Project Scope Statement

## Scope Summary

The Smart Building Automation project will implement a fully operational smart building automation system across Chaac University's 35 campus buildings to reduce energy consumption by 30%, improve facility management efficiency, and enhance occupant comfort. The foundation of this project is the installation of more than 5,000 IoT sensors and smart control systems, which will need to be integrated with the university's existing building management and HVAC systems. The project will include the implementation of a predictive maintenance system. Energy monitoring and optimization algorithms will be developed within this project.

The project also includes developing separate applications built on this foundation. A centralized management dashboard and mobile app will provide a single point for managing the system. The project will also involve the development of a student engagement app to raise sustainability awareness among students. This supports the sustainability initiative that drives this project. User training will be required for this project, particularly during later implementation phases. More than 50 facility management staff will be trained to use the centralized management dashboard and mobile app, the predictive maintenance system, and the energy monitoring and optimization algorithms, so that the university's facilities team can operate and manage the system once it is in place. The project excludes major building renovations or structural changes, non-automation facility upgrades such as furniture and landscaping, and dormitory automation, which is planned as a separate Phase 2 project.

---

## Project Deliverables

### System and Applications
* Fully operational smart building automation system across 35 buildings
* Installation of 5,000+ IoT sensors and smart control systems
* Integration with existing building management and HVAC systems
* Predictive maintenance system
* Energy monitoring and optimization algorithms
* Centralized management dashboard and mobile app
* Student engagement app for sustainability awareness

### Training and Documentation
* User training for 50+ facility management staff
* Comprehensive energy efficiency and cost savings report
* System maintenance guide and troubleshooting documentation
* Sustainability impact assessment and future roadmap

---

## Acceptance Criteria

### Performance Targets
* Energy consumption is reduced by 30% within 12 months
* Maintenance response time is reduced by 50%, as measured by work order logs
* System uptime and reliability are at or above 95%, measured monthly
* The university's carbon footprint is reduced by 25%, as verified by sustainability reports

### Deliverable Completion
* The smart building automation system is installed and fully operational across all 35 buildings
* The 5,000+ IoT sensors and smart control systems are integrated with the existing building management and HVAC systems
* User training is completed for 50+ facility management staff
* The energy efficiency and cost savings report, the system maintenance guide and troubleshooting documentation, and the sustainability impact assessment and future roadmap are delivered

---

## Project Constraints

### Schedule
* The project must be completed within 18 months, from the target start date of September 30, 2026, to the target completion date of March 30, 2028

### Budget
* The total pre-approved budget is \$3,200,000
* The project manager's spending authority is limited to \$3,000,000 within that budget
* All expenditure must follow university financial policies and be documented

### Scope
* The project cannot include major building renovations or structural changes
* Non-automation facility upgrades, such as furniture and landscaping, are excluded
* Dormitory automation is excluded and reserved for a separate Phase 2 project

### Technical
* The system must integrate with Chaac University's existing building management and HVAC systems, which are currently outdated and fragmented

---

## Project Assumptions

### Technical and Integration
* Installing the 5,000+ IoT sensors and smart control systems will not require major building renovations or structural changes
* Required technology components and software will be available and compatible with existing infrastructure
* The existing building management and HVAC systems will remain accessible and operational during integration
* The energy monitoring and optimization algorithms will depend on the continued functionality of the sensors and controls
* Data privacy and security measures will meet university and regulatory standards without causing project delays

### People and Vendors
* External vendors and contractors will deliver services according to agreed schedules and quality standards
* The university will provide access to all 35 buildings for installation
* The 50+ facility management staff will be available to attend user training
* Building occupants (faculty and staff) will participate in training and adopt the new system as intended

### Budget and Measurement
* The \$3,200,000 budget will remain available throughout the project and will be sufficient to cover all in-scope items
* Baseline data for energy consumption, maintenance response time, and carbon footprint will be available to measure the performance targets against
